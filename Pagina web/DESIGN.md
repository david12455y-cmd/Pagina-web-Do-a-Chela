## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, contemporary homage to the traditional Mexican *fonda* and *cocina económica*. It rejects kitsch tropes, relying instead on understated editorial minimalism where honest culinary craft takes center stage.

- **Brand Personality**: Warm, heritage-rooted, unpretentious, and artisanal. It balances the warmth of generational family recipes with the precision of high-end editorial curation.
- **Target Audience**: Discerning diners, culinary travelers, and locals seeking soulful, authentic regional Mexican dishes presented with modern clarity.
- **Emotional Response**: Grounded comfort, sensory appetite stimulation, and immediate trust in culinary authenticity.
- **Design Style**: Warm Minimalist Editorial. Generous breathing room, precise hairline rules, tactile warmth through nuanced ivory surfaces, and disciplined chromatic discipline to let rich moles, charred salsas, and heirloom corn hues dominate photography.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from Mexican culinary materiality—sun-baked clay, roasted agave, cured cornmeal, and golden canela broth.

- **Primary (`#A8422B`) — Terracotta Quemada**: A deep, mineral-rich earthenware red. Reserved for primary actions, badges denoting house specialties, active states, and editorial highlights.
- **Secondary (`#1C2421`) — Agave Charcoal**: An ultra-deep, blackened sage-charcoal derived from fire-roasted piñas. Serves as the high-contrast structural bedrock for body typography, structural borders, and dark hero banners.
- **Tertiary (`#DDA13E`) — Canela Amber**: A warm, toasted golden spice tone. Used sparingly for micro-accents, awards, ratings, price callouts, and subtle hover interactions.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF6EE`) — Masa Marfil**: An untreated, warm cornmeal ivory that creates a gentle, tactile canvas under natural light, preventing visual exhaustion common with stark pure whites.
- **Surface Elevation Hierarchy**:
  - `surface-base`: `#FAF6EE` (Masa Marfil canvas)
  - `surface-raised`: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp contrast for plate presentation cards)
  - `surface-sunken`: `#F2EBDC` (Pellicle tone for metadata tables and order strips)
  - `border-subtle`: `rgba(28, 36, 33, 0.08)` (Delicate agave boundary line)

## Typography

The type system creates deliberate tension between culinary heritage and functional modernity.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles**: **Playfair Display** introduces authentic Latin serif craftsmanship, featuring delicate bracketed serifs, rhythmic thick-thin stroke transitions, and a timeless editorial authority suited for dish titles, ancestral recipe stories, and menu sections.
- **Interface & Nutritional Body Copy**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides humanist geometric clarity. Its open counters and warm curves ensure effortless readability across ingredient breakdowns, allergen notices, ordering modals, and checkout workflows.
- **Editorial Conventions**: Dish names receive `headline-sm` with italicized regional notes. Metadata like spice levels, origins (*"Oaxaca, MX"*), and preparation times take `label-sm` set in small uppercase with +0.08em tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid governs desktop displays (max-width 1280px), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Rhythm & Cadence**: Layouts favor intentional asymmetrical balancing. Hero sections pair large photography blocks spanning 7 columns against clean text lockups spanning 5 columns.
- **Food Photography Showcase**: Dish cards utilize an edge-to-edge frame ratio within their container (4:3 or 1:1), surrounded by a perimeter padding of `space-md` or `space-lg` to create a matte-board framing aesthetic reminiscent of fine culinary monographs.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - Desktop: `>= 1024px` (Margins: `margin`, Gutters: `gutter`)
  - Tablet: `640px - 1023px` (Margins: `2rem`, Gutters: `1rem`)
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (Margins: `margin-mobile`, Gutters: `gutter-mobile`)

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic drop shadows, relying instead on **tactile tonal layering** and **whisper-weight hairline borders** to evoke pressed paper and ceramic table settings.

- **Tonal Stepping**: Surface separation is achieved by resting pure `#FFFFFF` cards on top of the `#FAF6EE` Masa Marfil canvas. This generates immediate, soft contrast that mirrors clean white porcelain over an unvarnished wood or linen surface.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines**: Cards, popovers, and sticky navigation bars feature a 1px border colored `rgba(28, 36, 33, 0.08)`.
- **Soft Ambient Halos**: For interactive overlays, carts, and floating menu panels, elevation is reinforced using a dual-diffusion ambient cast:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(28, 36, 33, 0.06), 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(168, 66, 43, 0.04);`
  The faint inclusion of the terracotta chromatic cast simulates natural light warmth reflecting off clay cookware.

## Shapes

The design uses **Soft (Level 1)** geometric profiling.

- Base interactive items (buttons, text inputs, segmented pills) use `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii.
- Food presentation cards and modal dialogs step up to `0.5rem` (8px).
- This restrained curvature imparts an architectural, handcrafted look, avoiding both cold industrial sharpness and overly playful, bubbly corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Terracotta (`#A8422B`) fill with pure white label typography (`label-md`). Hover transitions to a deepened clay tone (`#8E3520`) with smooth 200ms ease. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`, radius: `4px`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Transparent fill, 1px Agave Charcoal border (`rgba(28, 36, 33, 0.25)`), text in `#1C2421`. On hover: border and text shift to Terracotta (`#A8422B`) over an ultra-faint tinted wash (`rgba(168, 66, 43, 0.04)`).
- **Text Action**: Playfair italic link accompanied by a fine terracotta rightward arrow glyph with a subtle 2px underline offset.

### Dish Cards & Menu Items
- **Structure**: Surface-raised (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px `rgba(28, 36, 33, 0.08)` border. Top image container holds food imagery styled with warm saturation and natural depth of field.
- **Body Elements**: Dish title set in `headline-sm`, followed by a brief narrative ingredient listing in `body-md` (`#1C2421` at 80% opacity). Prices are emphasized using Canela Amber (`#DDA13E`) or Agave Charcoal with medium serif weight.
- **Pacing**: Generous inner card padding (`space-lg`) prevents photography from bleeding directly into typography.

### Chips & Tags
- **Dietary & Regional Chips**: Compact badges with `0.25rem` radius, colored in pale masa sunken background (`#F2EBDC`), `#1C2421` text, and uppercase `label-sm` tracking.
- **Specialty/Chef's Selection**: Tinted Terracotta ground (`rgba(168, 66, 43, 0.1)`) with `#A8422B` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields**: Background is pure `#FFFFFF` inset against `#FAF6EE` canvas. Border is 1px solid `rgba(28, 36, 33, 0.2)`. Active focus switches border to `#A8422B` with an outer glow of `0 0 0 2px rgba(168, 66, 43, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom square/circle tokens using Agave Charcoal contours; checked fill is Terracotta `#A8422B` displaying a crisp white micro icon.

### Culinary Storytelling Elements
- **Recipe Provenance Strip**: Full-width Agave Charcoal (`#1C2421`) module featuring warm ivory serif pull-quotes (`Playfair Display`, italic), anchored by subtle Canela Amber hairline dividers.
- **Daily Specials Board (*"Menú del Día"*)**: A vertical, stacked list component with dotted leader lines (`border-bottom: 1px dotted rgba(28, 36, 33, 0.2)`) connecting the ancestral dish name to its current market price.
